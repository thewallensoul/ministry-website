Catholicism

Guide in development. This resource will explain Catholic beliefs and practices, compare key claims with historic Christian perspectives, and offer help for thoughtful conversations.

Topics to develop

Understand Catholicism

Authorities, core beliefs, sacraments, key writings, and how Catholics describe their faith.

Compare claims

Examine points of agreement and disagreement with Scripture and other Christian traditions, with sources.

Conversation and exploration

Questions and resources for Christians speaking with Catholics and Catholics exploring the Christian faith.
