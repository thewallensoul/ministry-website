Other Religions

These guides are for anyone who wants to understand another religion, consider its claims alongside Christianity, or explore Christian faith from within that community.

Each guide will aim to describe the religion fairly, explain points of agreement and disagreement with Christianity, and offer respectful questions and resources for further study.
