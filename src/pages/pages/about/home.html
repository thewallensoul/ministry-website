About Computing Christianity

We are Matthew and Faith Jang. Computing Christianity exists to teach people who Jesus is, why Christianity is true, and how to live and think biblically.

We research important questions in depth, then organize the strongest evidence and resources into focused guides so people can study well without having to start from scratch.

The ministry

During college, we saw a need for more training in how to interpret the Bible, defend the Christian faith, and share the gospel. Discovering online apologetics radically strengthened Matthew's own faith. We want to help others find the same clarity and confidence.

Matthew is training and raising support to serve as a Ratio Christi chapter director. Lord willing, we plan to start a college club at OC in spring 2027, where students can explore questions about Christianity and learn to share their faith.

Through Computing Christianity, we are building free videos and focused study resources about Scripture, apologetics, and Christian living. They are for skeptics, people of other faiths, and Christians who want to grow. You can also find us on YouTube.
