Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This policy describes information that may be handled when you visit Computing Christianity, contact us, sign up for email updates, or give through our website. Some services are provided by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies.

Information You Provide

  • Email: If you email us, we receive the address and any name, message, or attachments you include. We use them to respond and keep the correspondence in our email account.
  • Newsletter: Our Kit signup form asks for your first name and email address. Kit stores subscriber information and sends the requested updates for us. You can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter.
  • Donations: Donations are handled through PayPal's Donate service. PayPal processes payment information and maintains transaction records. The website does not send your full card or bank details to a Computing Christianity application server. PayPal may provide us with transaction details and, depending on the donor's choices and account settings, identifying or contact information so we can administer the gift and keep financial records.

Website and Device Information

The current website code has no visitor accounts, application database, first-party analytics, or advertising pixels. The displayed monthly support progress is entered manually; it is not fetched from PayPal and does not display individual donor information.

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts. Our hosting provider may also process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. This can include an IP address, browser or device details, requested page, and time of the request. We do not control the hosting provider's server-log settings or retention period.

How We Use and Retain Information

We use information we receive to respond to messages, send requested newsletters, administer gifts, maintain appropriate financial records, and operate the ministry. We keep email correspondence and donor information available to us only as long as needed for these purposes and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Kit and PayPal also retain information under their own services, settings, and policies.

The website code does not set first-party cookies or use browser storage for visitor accounts or preferences. Third-party services, including PayPal and Kit, may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect technical or service-usage information when their features load or are used. Google may receive technical information when your browser requests its fonts and icons.

Third-Party Services and Links

  • PayPal Privacy Statement — donations and payment processing.
  • Kit Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum — newsletter signup and delivery.
  • Google Privacy Policy — fonts and icons loaded from Google Fonts.

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers as needed to provide the services described above. Links to other websites are governed by those sites' own privacy practices.

Your Choices and Requests

You can unsubscribe from newsletters using the link in any Kit email. For questions or requests about information held in our email or ministry records, contact us below. For information held by PayPal or Kit, you may also contact those providers directly through their privacy resources linked above.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Contact and Policy Changes

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@computingchristianity.com. We may update this policy as our website or information practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
